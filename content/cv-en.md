## Professional Summary

Ph.D. in Bioinformatics from Peking University with 20+ years of experience spanning software engineering, AI/ML platform building, computational infrastructure, and life sciences research. Previously worked at two precision medicine diagnostic companies, contributing to cancer genomic testing and infectious disease detection product development, as well as related research projects. Founded Beijing BioinfoBoost Technology Co., Ltd. as an OPC (One-Person Company) in 2023, applying AI and bioinformatics to the biomedical field. During this entrepreneurial journey, explored the development of LLM-based applications in scenarios such as genetic mutation pathogenicity interpretation. Adept at bridging the gap between data engineering, AI/ML, and biological research.

## Core Competencies

- **AI/ML Platform Engineering**: LLM API integration (OpenAI, DeepSeek), Prompt Engineering, RAG pipeline building, Agent workflow orchestration, model inference optimization & deployment
- **Software Engineering & Infrastructure**: Full-stack software development (Python, TypeScript, C/C++), Linux system programming, HPC environment management, Docker/K8s orchestration, data pipeline design (ETL/ELT), cloud services (Alibaba Cloud, AWS)
- **Drug Discovery & Genomics**: Multi-omics integration, biomarker discovery, cancer genomics, immunology & digital pathology, genetic variant interpretation
- **Platform & Team Leadership**: Built platforms from scratch as an OPC (CEO/Founder), led 20+ person research teams in prior roles, cross-team collaboration, matrix management

- Configured and managed cloud-based compute resources, including GPU or DCU instances from cloud providers, to support AI-related experimentation and development.

- Led a 20+ person research team collaborating with top-tier hospitals on cancer genomics, immunotherapy response prediction, infectious disease testing, and digital pathology.
- Developed and optimized multi-omics data integration and biomarker discovery pipelines (genomics, transcriptomics, metabolomics, epigenomics) -- building the computational infrastructure that enabled large-scale collaborative research.
- Built AI-assisted models for immunotherapy response prediction, contributing to 4 filed patents and multiple peer-reviewed publications.
- Managed cross-functional stakeholder relationships across clinical, scientific, and engineering teams, translating between domain-specific requirements and technical solutions.
